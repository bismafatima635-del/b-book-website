Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how B-Book may collect, use, store and protect information when you use the B-Book application and website.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, B-Book may collect account information such as your name, email address, profile information, business information, content you choose to upload, messages, and information needed to provide app functionality.

2. Device and usage information

We may process technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate location when a location feature is enabled, and usage or diagnostic information.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and improve B-Book, operate accounts, display relevant business content, enable communication, maintain security, prevent abuse, provide support, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Location

Location features are used only when enabled or otherwise permitted by the device and applicable permissions. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the app, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

B-Book is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so it can be reviewed.

8. Your choices

You may review or update certain account information through the app. You may also request account deletion using our Delete Account page.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit B-Book Support.
