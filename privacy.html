Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

B-Book ("B-Book", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, protected, and shared when you use the B-Book mobile application and related website.

By using B-Book, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, B-Book may collect information that you provide directly when creating or using an account.

This may include:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Account and profile information
  • Business name and business information
  • Business category and description
  • Profile or business images you choose to upload
  • Posts, reels, stories, comments, and other content you submit
  • Messages or other communications sent through B-Book
  • Information you provide when contacting support

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide the features and services of B-Book.

2. Device and Technical Information

When you use B-Book, certain technical information may be processed automatically to help operate, secure, and improve the application.

This may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Preferred language or language settings
  • General technical information
  • Diagnostic, crash, and performance information
  • Information about how features of the application are used

We use this information primarily for application functionality, troubleshooting, security, performance, and improvement.

3. Location Information

Some B-Book features may use location-related information when you choose to enable a location feature and provide the required device permission.

Location-related information may be used to support features such as discovering businesses or displaying location-related business information.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. If you do not grant location permission, certain location-based features may not work as intended.

4. User-Generated Content

B-Book allows users and businesses to create and share content. Content that you choose to make available through the application may be visible to other users depending on the feature and your account or privacy settings.

This may include business profiles, posts, images, videos, reels, stories, comments, and other information that you voluntarily publish.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly through your profile, posts, comments, stories, or other user-generated content.

5. Messages and Communications

If you use B-Book communication features, information contained in messages or communications may be processed as necessary to deliver those features and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

You should only share information with other users that you are comfortable communicating through the application.

6. How We Use Information

Information collected through B-Book may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Providing B-Book features and services
  • Creating and displaying business profiles
  • Displaying business-related content
  • Enabling communication between users and businesses
  • Providing location-based features when enabled
  • Improving application functionality and performance
  • Detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, spam, and misuse
  • Maintaining the security of accounts and the service
  • Responding to support requests
  • Investigating technical problems
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared or processed in limited circumstances, including:

  • With service providers that help us operate, maintain, secure, or improve B-Book
  • When you choose to make information or content publicly available through B-Book
  • When required by applicable law, legal process, or governmental authority
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users, B-Book, or others
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or other business transaction, where permitted by law

8. Third-Party Services

B-Book may rely on third-party services or technology providers to support functions such as authentication, hosting, storage, analytics, security, notifications, application operation, or other technical services.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services and subject to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, application, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide B-Book services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or fulfill other legitimate purposes.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

11. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your B-Book account and associated personal information through our account deletion page.

Visit: Delete Your B-Book Account

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes.

12. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Access certain information associated with your account
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account or personal information
  • Control certain device permissions, including location
  • Contact us regarding privacy-related questions or requests

Some requests may require verification to help protect your account and personal information.

13. Children's Privacy

B-Book is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through B-Book in violation of applicable requirements, please contact us. We will review the information and take appropriate action where required.

14. International Data Processing

Depending on the services and infrastructure used to operate B-Book, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to handle information in accordance with relevant legal requirements.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to B-Book, our practices, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, the updated policy will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
