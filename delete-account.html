Delete Your B-Book Account

You can request permanent deletion of your B-Book account and associated information by contacting our support team.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to our support team using the button below.

Request Account Deletion

What Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, B-Book will delete or permanently anonymize information associated with your account where technically applicable, including your profile information, business profile information, posts, comments, uploaded media, and other user-generated content associated with your account.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law or when reasonably necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of B-Book and its users.

Information retained for these purposes will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary.

Processing Time

We aim to review and process account deletion requests within 30 days.

Processing may take longer if additional verification is required or if certain information must legally or securely be retained.

What to Include in Your Email

Please send the request from the email address associated with your B-Book account and include your B-Book username or other information needed to identify your account.
